    How the art is made

    • Each category is a ribbon in its own colour, mirrored around the centre line.
    • Height along the timeline is how much of that time slice you spent on it, Gaussian-smoothed.
    • Ripple comes from intensity: harder sessions shimmer faster.
    • Particles are your energy over the last three hours.
    • Tempo follows how many entries you logged today.
    • Warmth of the background follows your average mood.
    • The seed fixes the noise field, so a saved piece can be re-rendered exactly. R rolls a new one.
